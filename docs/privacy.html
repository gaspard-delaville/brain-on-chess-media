Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026.

What data this app handles

The Brain_on_chess app connects to a single TikTok account (its own) via TikTok's standard OAuth login. The access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok are stored securely and used only to upload and publish videos to that same account through the Content Posting API.

The app does not collect, store, or share personal data belonging to other TikTok users. It does not read followers, comments, direct messages, or any other account's content.

Data retention

Authorization tokens are kept only for as long as needed to operate the account's own publishing, and are revoked immediately if the account disconnects the app from TikTok settings.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent via the Brain_on_chess social media accounts.
