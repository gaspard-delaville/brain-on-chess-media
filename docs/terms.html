What the app does

The app publishes chess videos (checkmate puzzles and famous chess games explained) created by the Brain_on_chess team to the Brain_on_chess TikTok account. It does not post on behalf of any other account, and it does not access, read, or repost content from other TikTok accounts.

This app uses TikTok's Content Posting API solely to upload and publish video content to the authorized Brain_on_chess account. Use of the app is subject to TikTok's own Developer Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.
